### Changed

- The agent avatar has a new look. The whole box has the agent's color, and its left side shows a
  symbol of the agent's role, which comes from the agent's title. The lid shows what the agent
  does: arrows while it works, a ring while it waits for you, and an × when it fails. While an agent
  works, points of light circle the box. When its chat is open, the avatar moves faster and the lid
  shows an equalizer. With a reduced-motion preference the avatar stays still.
